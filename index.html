The games

Out now

Moonrope

One finger. One rope. One night to sunrise.

Hold to catch a ring, let go to fly, and catch the next one before you fall. Swing through eight landscapes, from meadows and pine woods to a sea of clouds and the Kerala backwaters.

  • One-touch
  • Plays offline
  • Original music
  • Four modes

Felt Flight

A paper plane over a stitched felt world.

Hold to climb, let go to glide. Sew a red thread across the felt, bank buttons at pincushions, find rare animals and earn 26 felt badges. Made for all ages: no chat, no account, plays offline.

  • Five weather levels
  • Sticker book
  • Calm flight
  • All ages

Void Brawl

Six ships. One black hole. One survivor.

A neon arena brawler from the Dusk Moon Arcade. Host a match on your Wi-Fi and fight with up to five friends, no servers and no accounts. Ages 13+.

  • Up to 6 players
  • Local Wi-Fi
