Moonrope

One finger. One rope. One night to sunrise.

Hold to catch a ring, let go to fly, and catch the next one before you fall. Swing through seventeen skies from dusk to sunrise, as rings start to drift, hide and crumble.

  • Classic: climb to ring 100, then catch the tip of the moon
  • Endless: dusk to sunrise at ring 400
  • Sprint: race today's course to ring 50
  • Calm: no falls, just swinging and music

New players meet Lumi, a little crescent moon who shows you how to swing on your first run. Along the way you travel through eight landscapes, from meadows and pine woods to a village, snowy mountains, a sea of clouds and the Kerala backwaters, each with its own ground to land on. Watch for fireflies, owls, shooting stars and rare sights like the sky whale.

Felt Flight

A paper plane over a stitched felt world.

Hold to climb, let go to glide. Your paper plane sews a red thread trail across a stitched felt world. Collect buttons, bank them at pincushions for bonus points and felt patches, and open stitched gates to fly on from a sunny meadow, past a lake, an autumn forest and a windy coast to a stormy ocean.

  • Five levels of weather, from Calm to Storm, and beyond
  • Rare animals to find and collect in a sticker book
  • 26 felt badges to earn and share
  • Calm flight for younger players: no crashes, endless thread

Made for all ages. No chat, no account, plays offline.

Our story

Dusk Moon Studio began as a challenge to myself. Here is how it has grown so far.

  1. 27 September 2026

    Idea Barn

    A promise to myself: if I ever had to make the choice, how would I grow into a different world?

  2. Late September 2026

    From idea to studio

    Two small prototypes, a bubble game called Pressure and a rope swinger called Tether, and the name Dusk Moon Studio. Tether became Nightrope, then Moonrope.

  3. 1 October 2026

    Moonrope in closed testing

    Moonrope is approved for closed testing on Google Play.

  4. 1 October 2026

    Felt Flight is born

    A paper plane that glides on one touch. Two prototypes in one night, then a stitched felt world.

  5. 4 October 2026

    Felt Flight in closed testing

    Approved on Google Play three days after the first prototype.

  6. 11-13 October 2026

    Moonrope grows up

    Lumi the crescent moon, blended landscapes and twelve hidden secrets.

  7. 14 October 2026

    A home on the web, and a new idea

    duskmoonstudio.com is registered. Void Brawl begins: a top-down space arena with one black hole, six ships and one survivor.

  8. 16 October 2026

    Moonrope applies for production

    Moonrope+ is ready and the testing period is complete.

  9. 18 October 2026

    Moonrope: production granted

    Version 1.0.4 goes to Google for review.

  10. 19-20 October 2026

    Felt Flight: production

    Applied for production on the 19th, granted on the 20th.

  11. 21 October 2026

    Moonrope is live, Void Brawl testing begins

    Moonrope is on Google Play in 175 countries. Void Brawl goes out to its first testers.
