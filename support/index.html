Moonrope

How do I get a new version?

Google Play updates the game for you. When an update is ready, Moonrope also shows a "A new Moonrope is ready" pop-up on the menu; tap Update now to download it.

How do I replay Lumi's lesson?

Settings → Lesson → Show Lumi's lesson again.

How do I turn off game statistics?

Settings → Privacy.

The game feels slow on my phone.

Moonrope automatically lowers visual effects after a few seconds of slow frames. Closing other apps also helps.

I bought Moonrope+. How do I get it on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account. If it doesn't appear, email me and I'll help.

How do I reset my scores?

Clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall and reinstall. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Felt Flight

How do I get a new version?

Google Play updates the game for you. When an update is ready, the game also offers it on the menu.

How do I replay the first-flight lesson with Pip?

Open Settings and choose to show the lesson again.

How do I switch to portrait or landscape?

Settings → Screen → Portrait. Landscape follows the way you turn your phone.

The game feels slow on my phone.

Settings → Graphics → Normal. The game also switches to Normal by itself if the phone struggles.

How do I start at a higher level?

Open a level's gate by banking enough buttons in a flight. After that you can start there from Settings → Start from, using buttons from your jar.

I bought Remove ads. How do I get it on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account, then tap Settings → Restore purchase. If it doesn't appear, email me and I'll help.

How do I reset my scores?

Clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall and reinstall. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Something isn't working.

Close and reopen the game, and check that your phone has free storage. If it still happens, email me with your phone model and what you were doing.

Turntable coming soon

How do I play?

You never touch a block. Turn the whole table a quarter left or right with the two turn buttons, and every loose block slides away from you. Fill the glowing outline exactly to finish the figure.

How do I look around the table?

Drag on the table to see it from another angle. Looking around never moves a block.

How do I get three stars?

Finish the figure in no more turns than the number shown beside "3 stars in" at the top of the screen. Fewer turns is always better.

What do Undo and Hint do?

Undo plays your last turn backwards, and you get three undos on every table. A hint shows you a good next turn. You start with 3 hints, you can hold up to 5, and you earn one each time you finish a table with three stars for the first time. When you run out, the game offers a short ad you can choose to watch for more.

I ran out of turns.

Every table has a turn limit. You can restart the table, or choose to watch a short ad for 5 more turns.

How do I open the next island?

Finish 30 of the 36 tables on an island to open the next one.

What are the five worlds?

Steps teaches how blocks travel over walls and stairs. Pulse has pillars that rise and fall with every turn. Slots has gates that only let blocks pass one way. Tides and Vault mix everything together for a real challenge. Each world has its own colours and music.

Why do I see ads?

Turntable is free and supported by ads: a banner at the bottom, short full-screen ads at natural breaks, and optional ads you choose to watch for extra turns, hints or undos. There are no purchases at this time.

How do I share my island?

Tap Share on your island. The picture is made on your phone and handed to the app you pick. On Android it is also saved to your photo gallery.

How do I turn off music, sound or vibration?

Settings has three switches: Music, Sound and Vibration. Turn off any of them there.

How do I get a new version?

Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone) updates the game for you.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not yet. Your stars, figures, hints and settings are stored only on your phone, so a new phone starts fresh.

How do I reset my progress?

Clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall and reinstall. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Something isn't working.

Close and reopen the game, and check that your phone has free storage. If it still happens, email me with your phone model and what you were doing.

Void Brawl

How do I get a new version?

Google Play updates the game for you. When a new version is ready, Void Brawl also shows an "Update available" message on the menu. You can check any time from Settings → Updates.

How do I aim and fire?

Use the right stick. Move your thumb a little to aim only. Push out past the outer ring to fire. Double-tap and hold to fire your rope.

The game feels slow or warms my phone.

Settings → Graphics: set Glow and effects to LOW and Frame rate to 30. Closing other apps also helps.

How do I play with friends?

Tap Play Together. One player taps Host a game. Everyone else taps Join a game on the same Wi-Fi network and picks the host. Up to six players can join, and bots fill any empty seats.

I can't see my friend's game in the lobby.

Check that everyone is on the same Wi-Fi network and has the same version of Void Brawl (update on Google Play). If your phone asks to use the local network, allow it. Some guest or public Wi-Fi networks stop phones from finding each other; try a home network or a phone hotspot.

Do I need internet for Wi-Fi matches?

No. Wi-Fi matches run directly between the phones on your local network. There are no game servers. You only need a connection for ads and updates.

How do I see the whole arena?

Tap FULL ARENA at the bottom left during a match. Tap BACK TO SHIP to return to your ship.

How do I get an extra respawn?

In Classic, when you use your last respawn, you can choose to watch a short ad for one more respawn. It is offered once per match, and only when an ad is ready.

Why do I see ads?

Void Brawl is free and supported by ads: a banner on menu screens and a short ad after some matches. No ads appear during a match.

How do I reset my settings?

Settings → Reset settings, or clear the app's storage in your phone's settings. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
