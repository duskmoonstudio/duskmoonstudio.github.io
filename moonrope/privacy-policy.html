Moonrope – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Moonrope ("the app") is made by Dusk Moon Studio (Arun), Kerala, India. Contact: hello@duskmoonstudio.com.

What the app stores on your device

Your best scores, checkpoints, settings and whether you bought Moonrope+ are saved on your device. We do not run our own servers. Apart from the leaderboard scores described below, we do not receive this information.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services)

On Android, Moonrope offers leaderboards through Google Play Games Services. Signing in is optional: if you tap the leaderboard (trophy) button, Google asks you to sign in to Play Games. Once you are signed in, when you finish a run the app sends your score (or, for Sprint, your finishing time) for that game mode to Google, together with your Play Games player ID, so it can appear on the leaderboard. Other players can see your Play Games profile name and image next to your score, according to your Play Games profile and visibility settings.

This data is handled by Google, not by us: we do not receive your player ID, your name or the leaderboard data, and we do not run a server for it. If you never sign in to Play Games, nothing is sent. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and game data in the Google Play Games app or in your Google Account. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Game statistics (Firebase Analytics)

To see which parts of the game are too hard or too easy, the app sends anonymous game statistics to Google Firebase Analytics. These include:

  • game events, such as which mode you played, the ring you reached, your score, when you used a hint or a continue, and whether you shared a score card;
  • a random app instance ID created by Firebase (not your name, email or phone number);
  • basic device and app information, such as phone model, Android version, language, app version and approximate country or region (from your IP address).

We do not use these statistics for advertising or to identify you, and they are not combined with ad data. You can switch them off at any time in Settings → Advanced → "Share anonymous game stats" (in older versions of the app: Settings → Privacy). See Firebase's privacy and security information.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later in Settings → "Ad privacy choices" where it is offered. Owners of Moonrope+ do not see ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Moonrope+ is sold through Google Play (or the App Store). Payment is handled by Google (or Apple); we never see your payment details.

Rating the app

The app may ask you to rate it. The rating window is shown and handled by Google Play; we do not receive your rating through the app or any information about whether you left one.

Sharing your score

When you share a score card, the image is created on your device and passed to the app you choose. We do not receive it.

Children

Moonrope is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Do not sign in to Play Games if you do not want to use leaderboards; the game plays the same without it.
  • Turn off game statistics in Settings → Advanced (older versions: Settings → Privacy).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device. Leaderboard scores held by Google can be removed through your Play Games profile settings or by contacting us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: hello@duskmoonstudio.com.
