Tether – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Tether ("the app") is made by Dusk Moon Studio (Arun), Kerala, India. Contact: arunvt.dev@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

Your best scores, checkpoints, settings and whether you bought Tether+ are saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this information.

Game statistics (Firebase Analytics)

To see which parts of the game are too hard or too easy, the app sends anonymous game statistics to Google Firebase Analytics. These include:

  • game events, such as which mode you played, the ring you reached, your score, when you used a hint or a continue, and whether you shared a score card;
  • a random app instance ID created by Firebase (not your name, email or phone number);
  • basic device and app information, such as phone model, Android version, language, app version and approximate country or region (from your IP address).

We do not use these statistics for advertising or to identify you, and they are not combined with ad data. You can switch them off at any time in Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous game stats". See Firebase's privacy and security information.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Tether+ is sold through Google Play (or the App Store). Payment is handled by Google (or Apple); we never see your payment details.

Sharing your score

When you share a score card, the image is created on your device and passed to the app you choose. We do not receive it.

Children

Tether is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off game statistics in Settings → Privacy.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device.

Changes and contact
