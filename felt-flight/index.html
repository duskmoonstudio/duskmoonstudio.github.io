How to fly

Hold to climb, let go to glide

Your plane sews a trail of thread as it climbs. Climbing uses thread, so pick your moment and grab spools to top up.

Bank buttons for points

Buttons ride on your plane: heavier, but steadier in the wind. Glide over a pincushion to bank them. Bank 5 at once for ×1.5 points, 10 for ×2.

Loop the loop

Double tap to fly a full loop over a tall pin, or just for fun.

Thimbles are lives

Catch silver thimbles to fill up to three lives. A spare life puts your plane straight back in the sky.

Sew felt patches

Every 15 buttons you bank sews a patch onto your plane. It takes one hit from a pin, a leaf or the grass.

Five levels of weather

Calm, Breezy, Autumn, Gale and Storm. Wind bands lift you, push you down or slow you, and felt leaves drift across the sky.

For parents

Felt Flight is made for players of all ages, including young children.

  • No chat, no accounts, no sign-in. Players cannot talk to strangers or find other players.
  • One optional purchase. A one-time "Remove ads" is the only thing to buy. It's shown only in Settings, never during play, and Google Play can ask for your approval first.
  • Child-safe ads. The free game shows ads from Google AdMob. Every ad is requested as child-directed, limited to ads suitable for general audiences, and not personalised. The app does not use the phone's advertising ID.
  • No personal information collected by us. Scores and settings stay on the phone. We have no analytics and no servers.
  • Plays offline. With no internet connection the game still works; ads simply don't appear.
  • Gentle content. No violence, scary scenes or text chat. A crash just sends the paper plane fluttering down to the grass.

Common questions

How do I start from a later level? Finish a level once, then pick it under Settings → Start from.

How do I switch to portrait? Settings → Screen → Portrait. Landscape follows your phone as it turns.

How do I turn off music, sounds or vibration? Settings has a switch for each.

I bought Remove ads. How do I get it on a new phone? Sign in with the same Google account, then tap Settings → Restore purchase.

How do I reset my scores? Clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall and reinstall. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
