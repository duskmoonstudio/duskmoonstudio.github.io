Privacy Policy

Void Brawl: Space Arena · Last updated: 20 October 2026

Void Brawl: Space Arena ("the game") is made by Dusk Moon Studio, the trading name of Arun, an independent developer in Kerala, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. It applies to the Android app.

The short version

  • Void Brawl is made for players aged 13 and over.
  • There are no accounts, no chat and no online play in this version.
  • We do not collect, see or store your personal information. We have no game servers and no analytics.
  • The free game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software processes some technical information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first.

1. Information stored only on your device

The game saves your settings (sound, vibration, screen shake, aim guide, graphics options and your touch-button layout) on your phone. We never receive it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

2. Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, age, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location, and it has no sign-in. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, it shows banner ads on menu screens, a short full-screen ad after some matches (never during a match), and an optional ad that you can choose to watch to get one extra respawn in Classic mode. Ads are supplied by Google AdMob.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software processes some information when your phone is online:

InformationWhy
IP address (which can show your approximate area, such as country or city)To send the ad to your phone and to choose ads allowed in your region
Device and app information, such as phone model, Android version, language and the app's versionTo show an ad that fits your screen, to measure ads and to prevent fraud
Your Android advertising ID, if it is available and not switched offTo show and measure ads, and to personalise them where you allow it
Ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown, tapped or watched to the endTo count ads, pay us for them and give the reward for the optional ad
Performance and diagnostic information about the ad softwareTo keep ads working properly

We do not receive this information. Google's use of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other places where consent is required, the game shows Google's consent message before it asks for ads, and ads are limited if you decline. On your phone you can reset your advertising ID or switch off ad personalisation in Android Settings (Settings > Google > Ads). The game is not aimed at children under 13. If you want help with your ad choices, write to us.

4. Update check

When the game starts it may download one small file (version.json) from duskmoonstudio.com to see whether a newer version is available. Like any web request, this lets the website's host see your IP address. We do not use it to identify you, and no information about you is sent with the request.

5. Google Play

If you install the game from Google Play, Google may give us summaries such as install numbers, crash and performance reports and reviews, under Google's own policies. These do not tell us who you are.

6. Children

Void Brawl is made for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, please tell us and we will help.

7. Keeping and deleting information

We keep nothing about you on our servers. Information on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. For information held by Google for ads, use Google's own tools linked above.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be reflected in the Google Play listing.

9. Contact
