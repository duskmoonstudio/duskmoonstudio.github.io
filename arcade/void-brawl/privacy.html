Privacy Policy

Void Brawl: Space Arena · Last updated: 2 November 2026

Void Brawl: Space Arena (app ID com.duskmoonstudio.arena, "the game") is made by Dusk Moon Studio, the trading name of Arun, an independent developer in Kerala, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the game.

The short version

  • Void Brawl is made for players aged 13 and over.
  • There are no accounts and no chat. We run no online game servers; the game can play with others on the same Wi-Fi network, and that stays between your devices.
  • We do not collect your name, email address or other personal information.
  • The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to count how it is played (for example, which kinds of match are started) and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports. Neither tells us who you are. Where the law requires consent, they stay off until you have made your choice.
  • The free game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software processes some technical information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and on iOS it asks before tracking you.
  • We do not sell your information.

1. Information stored only on your device

The game saves your settings (sound, vibration, screen shake, aim guide, graphics options and your touch-button layout) on your phone. We never receive it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game does not ask for your email address, age, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location, and it has no sign-in. If you play over Wi-Fi, the game shows a player name and ship colour to the other players in the match; this stays between the devices (section 3). We do receive usage statistics and crash reports from Google Firebase, described in section 5. We do not use any other analytics or crash-reporting service.

3. Wi-Fi play

The game has no online servers. It can, however, play with other people who are on the same Wi-Fi network as you.

  • Wi-Fi play uses your local network (UDP) to find and connect to nearby players.
  • The game shows a player name and ship colour to the other players in the match.
  • This information goes directly between the devices on your network. It never goes to us.
  • Android and iOS may ask your permission for local network access before Wi-Fi play can find nearby players. If you say no, the rest of the game still works.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, it shows banner ads on menu screens, a short full-screen ad after some matches (never during a match; if you quit a solo match halfway, an ad may play before your next solo match), and an optional ad that you can choose to watch to get one extra respawn in Classic mode. Ads are supplied by Google AdMob.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software processes some information when your phone is online:

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other places where consent is required, the game shows Google's consent message before it asks for ads, and ads are limited if you decline. On iOS the game first shows Apple's "Allow tracking?" question; if you say no, ads stay non-personalised. You can change your ad consent choice later by opening Settings in the game and tapping the PRIVACY OPTIONS button (shown wherever consent is required). On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or switch off ad personalisation in Settings > Google > Ads. The game is not aimed at children under 13. If you want help with your ad choices, write to us.

5. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics, a service of Google, to understand how the game is played so that we can fix problems and improve it. It runs inside the game and sends statistics to Google, which processes them for us. The statistics do not include your name, email address or any other detail that says who you are.

InformationWhy
An app instance ID: a random number made for the game on your phone. It is not your advertising ID, and it is replaced if you uninstall the game or clear its storageTo count how many different phones play and how often, without knowing who they are
Device and app information, such as phone model, Android or iOS version, language, app version, and your approximate area (country or city) worked out from your IP addressTo see which phones and regions the game must work well on, and to find problems on particular devices

Firebase Analytics in this game does not use your advertising ID and does not use ad personalisation signals. Google keeps this data for a limited period (up to 14 months) and handles it under Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other places where consent is required, analytics stays off until you have answered Google's consent message, and it stays off if you decline. Because the app instance ID is random and we cannot link it to you, we cannot look up your statistics by name; uninstalling the game or clearing its storage starts a new, unconnected ID. If you have a question about analytics, write to us.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). We also use Firebase Crashlytics, another Google service, so that we receive a report when the game crashes and can fix the cause. A crash report contains your phone model, operating system version, the app version, and the technical stack trace (the list of program steps) at the moment of the crash. It uses the same random app instance ID as analytics, so it does not tell us who you are. Google holds this data for us and keeps crash stack traces and their identifiers for 90 days before it starts removing them. As with analytics, crash reporting stays off until you have answered the consent message where the law requires it, and it stays off if you decline.

6. Update check

When the game starts it may download one small file (version.json) from duskmoonstudio.com to see whether a newer version is available. Like any web request, this lets the website's host see your IP address. We do not use it to identify you, and no information about you is sent with the request.

7. Google Play

If you install the game from Google Play, Google may give us summaries such as install numbers, crash and performance reports and reviews, under Google's own policies. These do not tell us who you are.

8. Children

Void Brawl is made for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, please tell us and we will help.

9. Your choices and rights

Changing your consent. Wherever consent is required, you can reopen the ad consent choice at any time from Settings in the game (the PRIVACY OPTIONS button). You can also remove the game, or clear its storage, to start with a new random ID.

Your rights. You can ask us for access to information we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or object to how it is used. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR also gives you the rights to correct, restrict and move your information, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We hold no personal information about you on our own servers, and the statistics we receive carry only a random ID, so there is little we can look up, but we will answer every request. Write to us at the address in section 13. For information that Google holds for ads or analytics, you can also use Google's own tools linked above.

10. Transfers to other countries

Google processes the analytics, crash and ad data described above in India and in other countries, which can be outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. Google's own privacy information describes how it protects data when it is moved between countries.

11. Keeping and deleting information

We keep nothing about you on our own servers. Information on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Analytics statistics and crash reports are held by Google Firebase for a limited period (section 5). For information held by Google for ads or analytics, use Google's own tools linked above.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be reflected in the Google Play listing. On 24 October 2026 we added Google Firebase Analytics. On 26 October 2026 we added Wi-Fi play, Firebase Crashlytics, iOS advertising choices, and sections on your rights and international transfers.
