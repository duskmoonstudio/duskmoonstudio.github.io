Moonrope

One finger. One rope. One night to sunrise.

Hold to shoot your rope at the next ring and swing, let go at the right moment to fly, then catch the next ring before you fall. Rings start to drift, hide and crumble as you climb, and silver-blue bell rings play the next note of the music each time you catch one in a row.

Swing through seventeen skies from dusk to sunrise across eight landscapes: meadows, a lake, pine woods, a lighthouse coast, a village with lit windows, snowy mountains, a sea of clouds and the Kerala backwaters with a houseboat. Look for fireflies, owls and shooting stars, and rare sights like a comet, sky lanterns, a meteor shower and the sky whale.

  • Four ways to play: Classic (climb to ring 100 and catch the tip of the crescent moon), Endless (one night at ring 400 and beyond), Sprint (race today's course to ring 50 against the clock) and Calm (no falls, just swinging and music).
  • Lumi, a little crescent moon, shows new players how to swing on their first run. The lesson can be skipped and replayed from Settings.
  • Checkpoints every 25 rings, and sometimes a chance to continue from where you fell.
  • Nine ball colours, from Ember to the multicolour Aurora, and score cards to share with friends.
  • Moonrope+ is an optional purchase that removes ads and adds extras such as unlimited Calm sessions and free continues. Calm has three free sessions to try.

Original music, no account, and it plays offline. The game automatically reduces effects on slower phones. Scores, checkpoints and settings stay on the phone.

Felt Flight

A paper plane over a stitched felt world.

Hold to climb, let go to glide. Your paper plane sews a red thread trail across a stitched felt world, and every climb uses a little thread, so grab the spools floating by to keep flying. Glide over a pincushion to bank the buttons you carry for bonus points and soft felt patches that shield the plane from a hit.

Fly from a sunny meadow over a lake with felt boats, through an autumn forest and along a windy lighthouse coast, all the way to the open ocean under a grey storm sky. Bank enough buttons to open each stitched gate and the weather changes with you: Calm, Breezy, Autumn, Gale and Storm. Past Storm the plane keeps getting faster, so how far can you go?

  • Thimbles give you up to three lives, and a crash only sends the plane fluttering down to the grass.
  • Rare animals hide in the scenery. Tap their label to collect a sticker for your sticker book.
  • 26 felt badges to earn and share, kept on My shelf in the menu.
  • Button jar: spend the buttons you have banked to start a flight at a level you have already opened.
  • Calm flight: endless thread and no crashes, ideal for younger players.
  • Pip, a little golden felt button, shows new players how to fly on their first flight.

Felt Flight is made for all ages: there is no chat, no account and no way for players to contact each other. Scores and settings stay on the phone.
