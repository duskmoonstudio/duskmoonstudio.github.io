How it playsOne move. Endless twists.

You never touch a block. You turn the whole table a quarter left or right, and everything loose slides away from you.

01

Turn the table

Left or right, a quarter at a time. That is the only move.

02

Blocks slide and climb

03

Fit the glowing shape exactly, in as few turns as you can, for 3 stars.

InspirationBuilt on a beautiful idea

Hairline, by Lucas Marques

Turntable grew out of Hairline, a set of isometric line figures that answer the pointer, made by Lucas Marques. Its Turntable figure, blocks on a platter that settles on a quarter turn, became the heart of this game.

On this page, the keys nod to Hairline's Keyboard figure and the rising pillars to its Terrain figure. The drawings and code here, and everything in the game, are our own. Thank you, Lucas.
