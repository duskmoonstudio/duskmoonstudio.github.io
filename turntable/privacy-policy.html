Privacy Policy

TURNTABLE

3D BLOCK PUZZLE

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

Turntable: 3D Block Puzzle is made by Dusk Moon Studio (Arun) in Kerala, India. Questions about this policy: hello@duskmoonstudio.com.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, and we run no servers of our own.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which sends some data off your device.
  • An optional one-time purchase removes the ads. Google Play or the App Store handles the payment.

What the game stores on your device

Your stars and finished figures, your hint count, and your settings (music, sound, haptics) are saved on your device only. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage erases it.

Advertising GOOGLE ADMOB

Turntable is free and shows ads: a banner at the bottom, short full-screen ads at natural breaks, and optional ads you choose to watch for extra turns, hints or undos.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect your advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), device and app details, and how you interact with ads.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are used, and you can change that choice later where the option is offered.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the system may ask whether the app may track you. Ads still work if you decline; they are just not personalised.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Game statistics

Turntable does not use an analytics service. We do not collect statistics about how you play. If that ever changes, this page will say so first.

Sharing your island

When you tap Share, the picture of your island is made on your device and handed to the app you pick. We never receive it. On Android the picture is also saved to your photo gallery so other apps can open it.

Purchases

Turntable offers one optional in-app purchase, "Remove ads", which turns off banner and full-screen ads. Google Play or the App Store handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. We have no accounts, so we do not keep a record of who bought it.

The game remembers on your device that you bought it. If you reinstall or change phone, tap Restore Purchases in Settings and the store confirms your purchase. Refunds follow the store's own policy.

Links to other sites

The game links to this policy, to our website and to the Hairline project that inspired it. Those pages open in your browser and follow their own privacy policies.

Children

Turntable is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will help.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings.
  • Decline tracking on iPhone and iPad when asked.
  • Uninstall the game to delete everything it stored on your device.
  • For anything held by Google, use your Google Account settings or contact us and we will point you to the right place.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
