Our story

A promise to myself, and everything that has happened since.

  1. 27 September 2026

    Idea Born

    A promise to myself: if I am ever stuck, I will give myself permission to restart, step into the unknown, and grow into a brand-new world.

  2. 28 September 2026

    From idea to studio

    Two small prototypes, a bubble game called Pressure and a rope swinger called Tether, and the name Dusk Moon Studio. Tether became Nightrope, then Moonrope.

  3. 1 October 2026

    Moonrope in closed testing

    Moonrope is approved for closed testing on Google Play.

  4. 1 October 2026

    Felt Flight is born

    A paper plane that glides on one touch. Two prototypes in one night, then a stitched felt world.

  5. 4 October 2026

    Felt Flight in closed testing

    Approved on Google Play three days after the first prototype.

  6. 11-13 October 2026

    Moonrope grows up

    Lumi the crescent moon, blended landscapes and twelve hidden secrets.

  7. 14 October 2026

    A home on the web, and a new idea

    duskmoonstudio.com is registered. Void Brawl begins: a top-down space arena with one black hole, six ships and one survivor.

  8. 16 October 2026

    Moonrope applies for production

    Moonrope+ is ready and the testing period is complete.

  9. 18 October 2026

    Moonrope: production granted

    Version 1.0.4 goes to Google for review.

  10. 19-20 October 2026

    Felt Flight: production

    Applied for production on the 19th, granted on the 20th.

  11. 21 October 2026

    Moonrope is live, Void Brawl enters closed testing

    Moonrope is on Google Play in 175 countries. Void Brawl closed testing begins.

    Get it on Google Play
  12. Next

    Felt Flight launch

    The felt paper plane is cleared for production.
